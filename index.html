How to cause trouble.

Move: Arrow keys or A / D. Jump: Space; release early for a smaller hop.

Shoot: Hold Z or J. In boss fights, shots face the boss. In run-and-gun stages, they follow your facing direction. Hold Up or W to aim upward; combine with Left/Right to aim diagonally.

Dash: X or Shift. Pass through danger while dashing; wait a moment between dashes.

Parry: Press C or K in the air as you touch a pink projectile. A successful parry bounces you up and earns extra ink.

Switch weapons: Q or E swaps your two equipped weapons. Choose them in Loadout. Pressure Pen charges while you hold fire and shoots on release.

EX / Super: F spends one card for your weapon’s EX attack. V or L spends five cards for your equipped super. Hit enemies, parry pink shots, or equip Midnight Coffee to earn ink.

Flying: Arrow keys or WASD move freely. Space is not needed. C parries pink projectiles; X dashes.

Pause: Escape or P. R retries after a defeat. Gentle mode gives you 6 hearts and slower enemy attacks; Regular gives you 3.

Watch for the boss’s warning before each attack. Clear each island’s four opening stages to unlock its gate boss. Gate bosses unlock the next island and new supers. First clears give two coins; run-and-gun stages contain five more coins, banked on a successful clear. Replays never duplicate rewards. Campaign progress, purchases, loadout, and grades are saved on this browser.

On a phone, rotate to landscape and use the buttons below the stage. Enable sound from the top bar for original synthesized music and effects.
