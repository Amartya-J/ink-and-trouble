THE PLAYBILL

How the relay works.

  1. Create a room, then share its link or six-character code with one to seven friends. Everyone joins from their own device. The host can change the boss before starting.
  2. The host starts. Players take 35-second turns fighting the same boss. The boss keeps every hit between turns. Each player's damage score shows their contribution; everyone shares the win or loss.
  3. When it is your turn, move with arrows or A/D, jump with Space, hold Z to fire, dash with X, parry pink shots with C, use EX with F, and use your super with V.
  4. While watching, choose one assist per turn: a shield, a heart, or a full super meter. The fighter receives it live.
  5. Defeat the boss within three turns per player to win together. If everyone runs out of turns, the boss wins. The host can start a rematch.

On a phone, use the buttons below the stage. Landscape gives the best view. Your room is restored when you refresh this device.
