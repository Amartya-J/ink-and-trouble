# Design notes

The engine advances at 60 fixed steps per second; rendering uses requestAnimationFrame. Frame accumulation is capped after long frames, and losing focus pauses the game to prevent unfair damage. Intro, playing, paused, defeat, and victory are explicit states. The DOM owns menus; the engine owns combat.

Player hurtboxes are smaller than the artwork. Parry range is deliberately more generous. Projectiles are removed when consumed or offscreen. Damage protection prevents a volley from taking multiple hearts in a single frame. A final boss hit takes priority over same-frame damage. Dashes preserve their starting direction and grant protection for their short duration. Shots auto-face the boss so retreating and firing is practical.

Boss attacks use elapsed simulation time and a seeded generator for particles, making test runs repeatable. Each boss increases attack density at two health thresholds. Warnings appear before attacks, and phase transitions delay the next pattern. Victory tests use extended player invulnerability to isolate combat reachability; they do not establish human difficulty balance.

Save data is treated as untrusted: only known act/difficulty keys, finite positive times, and valid grades are accepted. Storage errors never prevent playing. No sensitive information is stored.

Art uses local Canvas 2D paths. Sound is generated by Web Audio only after a user gesture. There are no remote font, image, or music dependencies. To extend the game, add boss data and patterns in engine.js, illustration in art.js, and an act card in index.html, then expand act-bound validation and tests.
