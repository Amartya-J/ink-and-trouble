# Visual direction

Ink & Trouble opens as an illustrated picture-show poster. The room form occupies the quiet left side of the artwork. Joining a room switches to an arena and room controls; the title illustration never covers combat.

`art/picture-show.png` is original generated title artwork. The game itself retains its Canvas characters, maps and collision geometry. The small daisyUI control bundle is precompiled in `menu-components.css` and served locally. Its source is retained in `design/menu-components.input.css`; regenerate it with the Tailwind CSS 4 CLI and daisyUI 5.

References: Awwwards' games/entertainment collection and Gallery of the Gamer, used for an experience-first composition rather than copied assets.

Review criteria: visible primary action; cream/charcoal contrast; compact room state; separate desktop and mobile layouts; keyboard focus; reduced-motion styling. Build and multiplayer checks passed. Browser screenshot review was unavailable during this update and remains a verification limit.
